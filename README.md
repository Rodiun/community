# Rodiun — Community

**Take control of your AI stack.**

Rodiun makes lean, local-first developer tools — they show you what your AI is doing
and what it costs, collect nothing, and stay out of your way.

The first tool is **[Frugon](https://github.com/Rodiun/frugon)** — see where your LLM
bill leaks, on your machine. Local, honest, MIT.

## Get involved

- **Bugs & feature requests** → open an issue on the tool's repo (e.g. [Frugon issues](https://github.com/Rodiun/frugon/issues)).
- **More** → [frugon.rodiun.io](https://frugon.rodiun.io)
